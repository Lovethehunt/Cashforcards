Questions

Is there any cost to get an offer?

No. Offers are free and there's no obligation to sell.

How fast will I get an offer?

Within 24–48 hours of your submission, by email.

Who pays for shipping?

The seller pays for shipping. We don't provide prepaid labels. Once you accept your offer, we'll email you our shipping address. We recommend a tracked service and packing singles in sleeves and toploaders.

How do I get paid?

By PayPal, Venmo, Zelle or Cash App, after your cards arrive and we confirm they match your photos.

What if my cards arrive in different condition than pictured?

We'll send you a revised offer. You can accept it, or we'll return your cards.

What if you can't buy my cards?

If we pass on your cards and you checked the partner box, we may share your submission with a trusted partner buyer, who may email you their own offer. Any deal with a partner is between you and them. If you didn't check the box, your information stays with us.

What kinds of cards do you buy?

Pokémon, sports cards, Magic: The Gathering and other trading cards — as singles or in bulk. If you're not sure we buy it, send a submission and we'll let you know.
