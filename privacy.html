Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information cashforcards.app ("we", "us") collects when you submit cards for an offer, how we use it, and who we share it with.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, email address, preferred payout method, a description of your cards, bulk weight and count, and the photos you upload.
  • Technical details: your IP address and browser type, recorded with each submission to help prevent spam and fraud.

We don't ask for payment account numbers on this site. When you accept an offer, we'll ask by email for the details needed to pay you.

How we use it

  • To review your cards and send you an offer.
  • To email you about your submission, shipping and payment.
  • To keep records of our purchases.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and abuse.

We don't send marketing emails unless you separately ask to receive them.

Sharing with partner buyers

If we can't make an offer on your cards, and only if you checked the partner-sharing box on the form, we may share your submission (name, email, card details and photos) with a trusted partner buyer. That partner may contact you with their own offer. Partners agree to use your information only to make you an offer about that submission. Any deal you make with a partner is between you and them, and their own privacy practices apply.

If you didn't check the box, we don't share your submission with partner buyers.

Other sharing

We never sell your personal information. We use service providers to run this site: hosting and file storage (Vercel), a database (Neon) and email delivery (Resend). They process data only on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect against fraud.

Photos

Photos you upload are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses so we and, where you allowed it, partner buyers can view them. Please don't include personal documents or anything other than your cards in your photos.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions for as long as needed to make offers and keep business and tax records. Submissions that don't lead to a sale are deleted within [24] months.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to withdraw your partner-sharing permission, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. Withdrawing permission stops future sharing; it can't undo sharing that already happened.

Children

This site is for adults. If you're under 18, please have a parent or guardian submit for you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
